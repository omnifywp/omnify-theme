Asset Protection

2. Cryptographic Security & Anti-Piracy Delivery

Protect intellectual property with HMAC cryptographic signatures, strict expiration timestamps, and download attempt limiters.

SHA-256 HMAC Signed URLs

Download requests require a cryptographically verified hash parameter (hash_hmac('sha256')) based on unique customer access keys, order IDs, and timestamps. Direct file URLs are never exposed.

Configurable Expiry & Attempt Caps

Set global or per-product expiration windows (e.g. 48 hours) and maximum download attempts (e.g. 5 tries). Once exhausted, links automatically invalidate to prevent forum and link sharing.

Audit Logs & IP Tracking

Every single file download event is recorded in wp_omnify_downloads with exact timestamp, customer ID, IP address, and user-agent string for audit compliance.

Sales & Conversions

3. Frictionless Checkout & Order Fulfillment

Maximize checkout completion rates with 1-page modern checkout, automated abandoned cart recovery, and built-in fraud risk analysis.

Guest Checkout & Order Lookup

Customers can checkout instantly without mandatory account creation. A dedicated guest lookup portal lets buyers track status, access digital download tokens, and view invoices with Order ID & email.

Automated Abandoned Cart Recovery

Captures abandoned checkout sessions asynchronously via REST API. Configurable delay timers (default 60 mins), coupon incentives, and automated recovery email reminders win back lost sales.

Built-in Fraud Risk Detection

Automatic fraud status analysis detects IP country mismatches, disposable/temporary email domains, high-value transaction flags, and repeated checkout attempts.

Advanced Coupon Engine

Fixed cart discounts, percentage discounts, minimum spend thresholds, usage limits per user, category exclusions, and first-order-only promotion rules.

Order Management & In-App Refunds

Update fulfillment states (processing, shipped, completed), attach carrier tracking numbers, and process full or partial Stripe/PayPal/Razorpay refunds directly within WordPress.

Automated Customer Notifications

Built-in HTML email triggers for order confirmations, receipts, payment pending notices, download link access, shipping tracking updates, and refund receipts with full email logging.

Operations & Architecture

4. Analytics, Taxation & Developer Tools

Real-time reporting, granular regional tax rules, activity audit logs, and REST API keys for modern store operations.

Real-Time Sales Analytics

Track gross sales, net revenue, refund percentages, average order value (AOV), and digital asset delivery volume without slow third-party analytics trackers.

Configure country/state tax rates, tax-inclusive vs tax-exclusive pricing, tax-exempt customer flags, and customized delivery zone rate tables.
