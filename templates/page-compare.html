Head-to-Head Analysis

How OmnifyWP solves common pain points

VS WooCommerce

Eliminate database bloat & expensive extension fatigue

WooCommerce treats products and orders as standard WordPress posts. Under moderate traffic, queries against millions of rows in wp_postmeta grind checkouts to a crawl. OmnifyWP stores everything in purpose-built custom relational SQL tables, delivering sub-100ms response times without paying hundreds of dollars annually for essential checkout addons.

  • ✓ No wp_postmeta table fragmentation
  • ✓ Built-in coupons, reviews, abandoned carts & analytics
  • ✓ 100% theme-agnostic Gutenberg & Elementor blocks

VS Easy Digital Downloads

Physical merchandise & bundles without tiered surcharges

While EDD is respected for downloadable goods, expanding into physical merchandise, complex variant attribute swatches, or product bundles requires expensive recurring licenses or separate addons. Furthermore, EDD's free version levies up to a 2% platform transaction fee. OmnifyWP natively handles both physical and digital inventories with zero commission take rates.

  • ✓ Native support for physical parcel shipping & delivery zones
  • ✓ True 0% commission • keep 100% of your earnings
  • ✓ Bundled multi-gateway support (Stripe, PayPal, Mollie, Razorpay)

VS SureCart & Cloud SaaS

Full data sovereignty without third-party cloud lock-in

Cloud checkouts decouple payments by routing customer orders and customer records through external proprietary servers. If their servers experience an outage or change pricing terms, your business is compromised. OmnifyWP gives you the sleek checkout UX of a SaaS with the uncompromising security and ownership of self-hosted WordPress.

  • ✓ Zero cross-border data transit or GDPR exposure
  • ✓ No risk of 3rd-party SaaS subscription increases
  • ✓ HMAC-signed digital files remain on your secured server
