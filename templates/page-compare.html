Why switch from WooCommerce?

WooCommerce relies on WordPress's core post and postmeta tables, which forces large SQL JOIN operations as your orders and customer list scale into the thousands. OmnifyWP stores products and orders in purpose-built relational SQL tables, resulting in near-instant query lookups.

🛡️

Why not use an external SaaS?

External cloud checkouts stream your customers' sensitive transaction details to third-party databases and charge recurring subscription fees or transaction percentages. With OmnifyWP, 100% of your customer records and payment relationships remain securely on your own WordPress hosting.

🔒

Secure by cryptographic design

Unlike standard WordPress file downloads that expose direct media upload URLs, OmnifyWP signs every file request with an HMAC cryptographic token. Download links automatically expire after a set time window and enforce strict maximum download attempt counts.
