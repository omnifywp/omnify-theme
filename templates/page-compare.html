How OmnifyWP solves common pain points

VS WooCommerce

Eliminate database bloat & expensive extension fatigue

WooCommerce treats products and orders as standard WordPress posts. Under moderate traffic, queries against millions of rows in wp_postmeta grind checkouts to a crawl. OmnifyWP stores everything in purpose-built custom relational SQL tables, delivering sub-100ms response times without paying hundreds of dollars annually for essential checkout addons.

  • ✓ No wp_postmeta table fragmentation
  • ✓ Built-in coupons, reviews, abandoned carts & analytics
  • ✓ 100% theme-agnostic Gutenberg & Elementor blocks
